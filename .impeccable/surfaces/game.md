# Live hunt surface brief

Mode: Experience. Scope: `/game` and its camera, tracking, encounter, and recovery states.

Players must enter the hunt through an explicit gesture, recognize one of thirty
physical markers, understand whether a ghost is available, reserved, or cooling
down, and attack only a server-owned encounter. The live camera remains the
dominant surface. Essential state must stay legible in dim event lighting without
covering the marker, and every failure needs a direct recovery action.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** The phone becomes a night-hunt viewfinder with a field pass wrapped
around its edges, not a dashboard placed in front of a camera. Refuse a centered
game card and decorative AR chrome that obscures the physical marker.

**OWN-WORLD:** Real camera imagery owns the field. Ink-black violet edge sheets,
bone-white type, one safety-orange attack action, cyan live signals, lavender
rules, compact monospace telemetry, and restrained 12px controls inherit the
Night-Hunt Field Pass without faking a sci-fi targeting display.

**STORY:** Grant camera access, sweep a printed marker into view, let the server
claim and assign its ghost, keep the marker visible while attacking, then see the
verified score and shared cooldown. Tracking loss pauses interaction and asks for
reacquisition; camera interruption offers a deliberate resume.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** The camera fills the phone. A narrow safe-area HUD pins timer
and score across the top, tracking instruction sits near the lower third, and the
single attack control rests above the bottom safe area. Permission, unsupported,
and recovery states replace the camera with one clear action and exact guidance.

**FORM:** Code-led Experience surface extending the established world, first in
the live-play implementation order. `seed: none (unavailable)`. The signature
interaction is a stable physical marker resolving into a server-selected ghost
that floats on the marker and recoils only after an acknowledged hit.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the
finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its
provenance
